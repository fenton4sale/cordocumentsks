Allegations Summary

The COR Cover-up allegations concern Church of the Resurrection’s alleged handling of reports of sexual misconduct involving Scott P. Moore at youth retreats. This page presents the allegations as documented in the attached court filings, transcripts, appellate opinion, and summary document. It does not state that every allegation was judicially proven.

The central allegation is that, in 2005, teenage campers reported nighttime sexual touching or attempted sexual touching at a Church of the Resurrection youth retreat in Linn County, Kansas, and that the church responded internally rather than immediately referring the matter to law enforcement.

The alleged cover-up includes claims that church officials investigated the matter themselves, interviewed alleged victims and witnesses, and failed to notify police at that time. The State’s court filing also alleges that camp leadership reacted dismissively, including characterizing the boys’ story as “shaky” and giving a sermon about people who spread rumors.

The alleged enabling issue is that Moore was allowed to serve again as a cabin leader at the 2006 annual retreat despite the 2005 reports and internal investigation. Court documents and the Kansas Court of Appeals opinion describe additional allegations from 2006 involving similar nighttime conduct with teenage boys.

The document set below includes the COR allegations summary, trial transcripts, the appellate opinion, the defense motion for reconsideration, and the State’s response.
